Manual UPI verification

The static UPI QR cannot be verified by the gateway. If a customer pays to it, check your bank/UPI app for the exact amount and reference, then use Mark paid with that reference. Only do this after you have personally confirmed the credit — it unlocks the file immediately. With Razorpay connected, orders unlock automatically and never need this.
