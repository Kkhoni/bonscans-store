The collection

Every guide, sold on its own.

Each ebook has a separate checkout and a separate delivery. Pay for one, unlock one — your link only ever opens the ebook you bought.

Prices are in Indian Rupees (INR). Payment by UPI, QR, card or netbanking.

How it works

Four steps from browsing to reading.

1 · Choose your ebook

Each card is a complete, separate product. Open its checkout to see exactly what you’re buying and what it costs.

2 · Pay securely

UPI, UPI QR or card through the payment gateway. The amount is fixed to that ebook’s listed price.

3 · We verify it

Our server checks the gateway signature and the paid amount before anything unlocks. No screenshot-based unlocks.

4 · Read or download

Your success page opens the ebook in a browser reader or downloads the PDF straight to your device.
