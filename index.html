Ask us a question
How will I receive my ebook after paying?

As soon as the payment gateway verifies your transaction, the store unlocks your ebook and sends you to a success page with two buttons: Read ebook online and Download ebook. No waiting for an email.

Can I pay with UPI or a QR code?

Yes. Checkout supports UPI, UPI QR, cards and netbanking through Razorpay. The QR shown on the checkout page is the store’s personal UPI QR — payments made to a static QR need manual verification by us, whereas the gateway checkout verifies and unlocks automatically.

Where can I download my ebook again later?

Keep the purchase link from your success page, or open My purchases on the same device. Your confirmation page regenerates fresh, secure download links each time.

Can I buy more than one ebook?

Each ebook is a separate product with its own checkout and price. Buy them one at a time — every purchase unlocks only the ebook you paid for.

Do the ebooks expire?

No. The files are yours. Only the individual download links expire for security; reopen your purchase page to create new ones.

What if my payment fails or money is deducted?

Failed payments never unlock an ebook. If money left your account without a confirmation, contact us with your order reference and we’ll verify it against the gateway records and fix it.
